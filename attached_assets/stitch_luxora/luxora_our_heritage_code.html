The Visionaries

Founders' Legacy

In 1894, Arthur and Elena Luxora established their first atelier in the heart of Geneva. Driven by a singular obsession with chronometric perfection, they didn't just build watches; they engineered time itself. Every piece was a testament to their belief that a timepiece is a living vessel of history.

"Time is the ultimate canvas."

— A. LUXORA, 1902

A Century of Precision

1894

The Geneva Inauguration

Opening of the first Luxora workshop. The creation of the 'Calibre Zero', our first in-house movement.

1945

The Golden Era

Post-war innovation leads to the introduction of the 'Horizon' series, setting new standards for maritime navigation.

2024

Heritage Reimagined
